### 5. Exact symbol equality (plan Step 2.1)

- Jeff's point: the trailing-zero hack was there because of IPL-V's "invisible zeros". The
  manual: "Represent the character A by the IPL-V symbol A0 (A-zero)". So the only real implied
  zero follows a **lone region character** (`A` ≡ `A0`). Termination 0 also arrives as `""` or
  `"0"`. Blank padding belongs to data-term contents, which is J114's job. The hack generalized
  this to "trailing zeros never matter", which made `Q1` = `Q10`, `K2` = `K20` and `9-12` = `9-120`.
- **Measured before changing anything**: wrapping `ipl-string-equal` showed 81,024 calls in LT,
  1,307 in the HC, 79 in the annexer, 5,742 in Yngve and 6,422 in clean Yngve, with **zero**
  calls where the old and exact answers differed. The bug is latent in all current programs.
- New `tests/symprobe.card` (test `symbols`):
  - R1-R6: J2 tests. R1 `Q1`/`Q10`, R2 `Q1`/`Q1`, R3 `K2`/`K20`, R4 locals `9-12`/`9-120`,
    R5 `A`/`A0`, R6 `A01`/`A1`.
  - R7/R8: J10 on a description list holding Q10 before Q1.
  - Before the fix: R1, R3 and R4 gave T, and R7 found `V10`.
- Fix: `canonical-symbol` maps `""`/`"0"` to `0` and a lone non-digit `X` to `X0`, and
  `ipl-string-equal` is then `string=`. The J2 comment was updated.
- After the fix all probes are right and `regress.py` passes 8/8 with no program output changed.
  With the fix stashed, `symbols` fails on exactly R1, R3, R4 and R7.
- `A01` ≠ `A1` both before and after. Whether IPL-V's number part ignores leading zeros was not
  checked.
