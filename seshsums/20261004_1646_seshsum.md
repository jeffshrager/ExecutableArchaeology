## Work Done

### 1. What the Lisp print-line J's do (read from `IPL-V/iplv.lisp`)

- **J157** enters a data term at its stored length (alphanumeric fragments are stored
  without trailing blanks). If the term doesn't fit by column 80, it enters **nothing** and
  sets H5−. This is what makes word-boundary wrapping possible without knowing word lengths.
- J154 clears the line and sets 1W25 = 1. J155 prints the line after a row of colons. J161
  advances the column (J157 fails later if it passes 80).
- J71 is a no-op in Lisp. J100 stops with H5− as soon as its subprocess returns H5−. J61 on
  an empty list returns its input and H5−. J90 makes a new empty list.

### 2. `SimonYngve2/ysimon-clean.card`, built by `make_clean_deck.py`

The deck is generated from the faithful `ysimon.card`, following the adapt.py pattern, and
its type-1 comment cards list the changes:

- The four unnumbered trace cards are removed (40H0/J152 in C1, 40H0/J153 in C2).
- **C3 is replaced by C3–C9:**
  - **C3**: build the units of the sentence (L1) on L3 (J100/C4); J65 the period N2 onto
    the last unit (J61, 52H0); J154; print each unit (J100/C5); J155 the last line; J154
    J155 for a blank line; erase the units (C9); empty L3, L4 and L1 with `J75 J71`.
  - **C4** (per word): if the word is B37 `,` or B47 `S`, the target is the last unit on L3;
    otherwise J90 makes a new unit and J65 adds it to L3. Then J50 puts the target in W0, J100
    with C6 copies the word's fragments into it, and J30 restores W0.
  - **C5** (per unit): enter its fragments with `10J157 / J100`. On H5−: J154, rebuild the
    line from L4 (C7 = C8 + a space), J155, J154, empty L4, then C8 the unit. Then a space
    (`10N1 J161`) and J65 the unit onto L4.
  - **C6** J65 (0) onto list W0. **C7** C8 plus a space. **C8** `10J157 / J100`. **C9** J71.
- B41 `PROUD.`: the fragment `D.` becomes `D`.
- New data: N2 `+21.` (the period); L3 and L4 empty lists.

It worked on the first run. Checks:

- With seed 53, the 20 sentences equal the faithful run's `sentences.txt` after normalizing
  ` S`→`S`, ` ,`→`,` and `PROUD.`→`PROUD`, with a period added.
- The longest line is 80 characters, and no word is split.
- The `--count 3 --seed 7` run works.

Not fixed, because it is in the grammar: the plural is a bare S (`FIREBOXS`), and articles
aren't adjusted (`A OILED`).
